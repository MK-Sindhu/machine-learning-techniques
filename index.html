How the course is laid out

The course starts with unsupervised learning and then moves to supervised learning. Sequential learning (online learning, bandits, reinforcement learning) is mentioned in Part 1 but not taught.

Using these notes

  • Every section starts with a timestamp chip, such as L6 · 12:40, that opens the video at that moment.
  • Each part ends with key terms and questions whose answers stay hidden until you open them.
  • Charts built on synthetic data say so in their caption. They illustrate the idea, not a dataset from the course.
  • The course's own captions exist for most lectures up to L45. L36, L38, L40 and everything from L46 on only have YouTube's auto-generated captions, so those notes rebuild formulas from context; each page's footer says which captions it used.
  • The notes follow the lectures, not a textbook. For more depth the lecturer recommends Bishop's Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning.
